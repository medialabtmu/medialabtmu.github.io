abstract: "EEG-based emotion recognition systems face significant challenges when deployed across different domains due to limited labeled data and individual signal variability. We introduce a source-free unsupervised domain adaptation technique that operates without requiring access to original training data — a key advantage for privacy-sensitive applications. Our framework minimizes prediction discrepancies on confident samples and enforces local consistency by promoting similar predictions among reliable neighbours. Evaluated on DEAP, SEED, and DREAMER benchmarks, the method achieves substantial improvements over existing approaches."
