Md Niaz Imtiaz and Dr. Naimul Khan's paper **"Towards Practical Emotion Recognition: An Unsupervised Source-Free Approach for EEG Domain Adaptation"** has been accepted in [IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/RecentIssue.jsp?punumber=5165369).

The paper addresses the challenge of deploying EEG-based emotion recognition across different subjects and datasets, where distribution shift and limited labeled data are major barriers. The proposed source-free domain adaptation framework requires no access to the original training data — an important advantage for privacy-sensitive applications. The method enforces consistency among confident predictions and their local neighbours, achieving state-of-the-art results on the DEAP, SEED, and DREAMER benchmarks.

Preprint and code are available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.03707). Congratulations Niaz!
