Dr. Naimul Khan led the **IMMERSE Innovation Pathway**, bringing together 7 students from the Faculty of Engineering and Architectural Science (FEAS) and The Creative School at Toronto Metropolitan University. Working in 4 interdisciplinary teams with Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre PIs, the students tackled real-world healthcare challenges over the past year.

Their work was showcased at the **SCSC Immerse: Ideas in Motion** event at Sunnybrook's Canadian Simulation Centre — an event timed to Healthcare Simulation Week, celebrating the intersection of simulation, experiential learning, and healthcare innovation.

---

TMU's Provost and incoming President [Dr. Roberta Iannacito-Provenzano](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dr-roberta-iannacito-provenzano-4994831b_healthcareinnovation-experientiallearning-ugcPost-7505780556958347264-bvNN/) shared her reaction after attending the showcase:

> *"I had the pleasure of attending the SCSC Immerse: Ideas in Motion Showcase at Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre to witness our TMU students in action with their Sunnybrook partners. One year in, the momentum is inspiring and the possibilities ahead are even more exciting."*

Congratulations to all the student teams, and thank you to our partners at Sunnybrook — Agnes Ryzynski, Dr. Calvin Law, and Andy Smith — for making this collaboration possible.
