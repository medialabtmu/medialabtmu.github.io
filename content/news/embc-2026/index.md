RML is proud to have three papers accepted to the [IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Conference (EMBC 2026)](https://embc.embs.org/2026/), taking place in **Toronto, Canada** in July 2026.

**MultiLangCue: Exploring Multilingual Emotion Recognition Through Language-Specific Prompting**
*N. Vieira, C. Wasef, N. Khan*
This paper investigates how emotion recognition models can be adapted through language-specific prompting, leveraging features unique to each language. The approach is evaluated on English and German datasets, exploring the intersection of multilingual NLP and affective computing.

**R-ECAM: Reliability-Aware Ensemble Class Activation Map for Breast Lesion Pseudo-Mask Generation**
*S. Barzegar, N. Khan*
An unsupervised segmentation model for breast lesion detection that leverages class activation maps to generate reliable pseudo-masks — enabling weakly-supervised training without dense pixel-level annotations.

**In-Context Facial Expression Synthesis for Sign Language Generation Using Large Language Models**
*S. Hassan, N. Khan*
A novel approach to facial expression synthesis for sign language generation using in-context learning with large language models — an underexplored problem with important implications for accessible communication technology.

Congratulations to Nick, Carol, Somayeh, and Safwan!
