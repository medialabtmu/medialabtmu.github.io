## Overview

In partnership with [Kids Help Phone](https://kidshelpphone.ca/), RML is developing a generative AI simulator for crisis responder training. The system is trained on 750,000+ de-identified KHP transcripts to generate realistic youth crisis scenarios, enabling volunteers to practice in a safe environment with real-time performance feedback aligned with KHP's clinical standards — before working directly with young people.

The prototype will be tested internationally and shared globally through [Child Helpline International](https://childhelplineinternational.org/). Young people serve as co-applicants and participate in prototype development to ensure cultural and linguistic accuracy.
